## What it is

Sales records the amounts a company expects from, or has received from, its customers. It is the customer-facing side of the product's [shared concepts](concepts.md): a customer is the contact that receives the sales record, and an invoice is the document that states the amount due for goods or services.

An estimate records a proposed sale before it becomes an invoice. An invoice lifecycle is the changing state of that invoice as its amount becomes due and is then settled. A recurring invoice represents an invoice pattern that repeats over time. These sales document concepts are described by the [Invoices and estimates Help Centre reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/invoices-estimates/).

## Main capabilities

- A **customer** is the customer-side contact connected to sales documents.
- An **estimate** expresses a proposed customer charge before it is recorded as an invoice.
- An **invoice** records the amount a customer owes for a sale.
- The **invoice lifecycle** distinguishes an invoice's outstanding amount from an invoice that has been settled.
- A **recurring invoice** represents a sales invoice that is expected to repeat.
- A **payment** records settlement against an invoice; a **split payment** records that settlement in more than one part.
- An **online payment** is a payment made through an online payment option associated with the invoice.

The [Payments Help Centre reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/payments/) describes payments as the settlement counterpart to invoices, including partial or split settlement and online payment options. Sales is a product area in the [navigation menu reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/the-user-interface/navigation-menu/).

## Related concepts

The principal sales relationship is customer -> estimate or invoice -> payment. A payment can settle an invoice in full, as a split payment, or through an online payment option. The common labels for contact, document, and payment are defined in [concepts](concepts.md).

When a payment has a banking counterpart, [banking](banking.md) explains the transaction connection. [Reporting](reporting.md) analyzes the sales records and their resulting activity rather than replacing the invoice or payment. These relationships follow the [Invoices and estimates](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/invoices-estimates/) and [Payments](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/payments/) product references.
