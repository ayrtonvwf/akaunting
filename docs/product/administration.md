## What it is

Administration is the cross-cutting vocabulary for configuring a company, finding product areas, controlling access, and exchanging data. A **company** supplies the working context for its records and settings, while dashboard **widgets** provide configurable overview views of that activity; the [Settings](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/settings/) and [Help Centre category index](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/) provide the source context for these areas.

## Main capabilities

- The **navigation menu**, **sidebar**, **search**, **favorites**, and **quick creation** vocabulary describes how people move among product areas, retain useful destinations, find records, and begin creating a record. The [navigation menu](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/the-user-interface/navigation-menu/) and [sidebar](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/the-user-interface/the-sidebar/) references are the source context for the interface terms.
- **Company** and **localization** settings establish the company context and its regional presentation. **Defaults**, **invoice**, **email-template**, and **schedule** settings provide reusable configuration for records and communications, while **category**, **currency**, and **tax** settings define shared classification, valuation, and tax-treatment vocabulary. These settings affect the records that [sales](sales.md), [purchases](purchases.md), and [banking](banking.md) describe; see [Settings](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/settings/).
- A **user** is a person with access to a company. A **role** groups access expectations, and **permissions** determine which product areas or actions that role can use; the **client portal** is the customer-facing access vocabulary associated with sales records. The [roles and permission levels reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/users-and-roles/roles-and-permission-levels/) is the source context for access vocabulary.
- An **app** is a capability discovered, installed, subscribed to, or updated through the product's apps vocabulary. Apps may add product capabilities, so app terminology connects the core reference to [optional apps](optional-apps.md) without treating those optional capabilities as core.
- **Import** and **export** describe data interchange: import brings externally prepared data into the product context, and export produces data for use outside it. The [Help Centre category index](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/) lists Import and Export as its own product area.
- **On-premise** describes installation vocabulary for a self-managed product deployment, and an **API key** identifies the credential vocabulary for application access. **Cash basis** and **accrual basis** distinguish report interpretations of recorded activity; see [reporting](reporting.md) for their shared reporting meaning and the [Help Centre category index](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/) for the On-Premise, Developers, and FAQ areas.

## Related concepts

The company context owns the shared categories, currencies, taxes, and access arrangements used across the product. Navigation and creation vocabulary reaches the domain records described in [sales](sales.md), [purchases](purchases.md), and [banking](banking.md), while dashboards and [reporting](reporting.md) summarize recorded activity rather than replace it. The canonical shared terms and their relationships are in [concepts](concepts.md); [Settings](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/settings/) and [roles and permission levels](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/users-and-roles/roles-and-permission-levels/) provide the Help Centre context for settings and access.
