## What it is

Banking records money movement in the company’s accounts. Sales and purchase documents describe amounts that are owed; banking records the account activity that receives, pays, or otherwise moves money. [Bank feed and reconciliation](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/banking-feeds-reconciliations/) is the Help Centre source context for this area.

## Main capabilities

- An **account** is the place within the product where banking activity is recorded.
- A **transaction** records a movement of money in an account.
- **Income** is an account transaction that records money received.
- An **expense** is an account transaction that records money paid out.
- A **transfer** records movement of money between accounts.
- A **bank feed** is account activity supplied from a connected bank source for review as banking records.
- **Reconciliation** relates recorded account activity to the records used to account for that activity.
- A **split transaction** divides one transaction into more than one recorded part.
- A **document connection** links a banking transaction with the sales or purchase document whose settlement it represents.

The [managing transactions reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/banking-feeds-reconciliations/managing-transactions/) provides the source context for transaction vocabulary and its relationship to account activity.

## Related concepts

Banking records money movement and reconciliation relates account activity to those records. In contrast, [sales](sales.md) uses customer -> invoice -> payment to describe money owed by customers, and [purchases](purchases.md) uses vendor -> bill -> payment to describe money owed to vendors. A payment can have a document connection to the banking transaction that records its movement in an account.

[Reporting](reporting.md) presents views over the recorded banking, sales, and purchase data; it does not replace the underlying transaction or document. Shared terms such as company, document, and payment are defined in [concepts](concepts.md).
