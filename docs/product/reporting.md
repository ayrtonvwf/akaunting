## What it is

Reporting presents reports as views over recorded product data. A report organizes the recorded banking, sales, purchase, and shared accounting data for a chosen question; it does not create or replace the underlying documents and transactions. The [Reports Help Centre reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/reports/) provides the source context for the reporting area.

## Main capabilities

- A **report type** identifies the kind of recorded data and summary a report presents.
- A **period** is the time span used to select recorded data for a report.
- **Grouping** arranges report data into related sets for reading the summary.
- **Cash basis** presents activity according to recorded money movement.
- **Accrual basis** presents activity according to the recorded obligations and related activity rather than only money movement.
- **Filtering** narrows a report view to records matching selected conditions.
- **Pinning** keeps a report available as a saved point of reference.
- **Printing** produces a readable copy of the report view.
- **Export** produces a portable copy of the report data or view.

Income and expense reports, profit-and-loss reports, and tax-summary reports are standard reporting vocabulary. The [accessing and viewing reports reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/reports/accessing-and-viewing-reports/) and [creating a new report reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/reports/creating-a-new-report/) describe the Help Centre context for working with report views and report types.

## Related concepts

[Banking](banking.md) records account movements, while [sales](sales.md) records customer -> invoice -> payment and [purchases](purchases.md) records vendor -> bill -> payment. Reports present views over those recorded data rather than changing the underlying account transactions, documents, or payments.

The shared meanings of company, category, currency, tax, document, and payment are defined in [concepts](concepts.md). [Optional apps](optional-apps.md) can provide installed report types beyond the core reporting vocabulary described here.
