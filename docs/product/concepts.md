## What it is

Akaunting records financial activity within a **company**, the working data context that owns the records used across the product. Its shared vocabulary connects sales, purchases, banking, settings, and summaries; see the [Help Centre category index](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/) and the [navigation menu reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/the-user-interface/navigation-menu/) for the product areas that organize those concepts.

- **Company** is the working data context that owns the records and settings discussed in this reference.
- **Dashboard** is a configurable overview that brings recorded activity into view; [administration](administration.md) describes its place in the product.
- **Item** is a reusable thing or service that can appear on both sales and purchase documents; see [sales](sales.md) and [purchases](purchases.md).
- **Contact** is a party used in product records, including customers on the sales side and vendors on the purchasing side; see [sales](sales.md) and [purchases](purchases.md).
- **Category** classifies activity across domains so related records can be grouped; see [administration](administration.md).
- **Currency** supplies the monetary unit used to value activity across domains; see [administration](administration.md).
- **Tax** classifies tax treatment across domains and contributes to the value recorded on relevant documents; see [administration](administration.md).
- **Document** is a business record such as an invoice or bill that captures a sales or purchase obligation; see [sales](sales.md) and [purchases](purchases.md).
- **Payment** records settlement of an amount associated with a document; see [sales](sales.md), [purchases](purchases.md), and [banking](banking.md).
- **App** is an installable product capability that can add functions to the product; see [administration](administration.md) and [optional apps](optional-apps.md).

## Main capabilities

The core reference follows the product areas listed by the [Help Centre category index](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/). A company’s activity can be entered as sales, purchases, or banking records, with category, currency, and tax providing shared classification or valuation context; [settings](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/settings/) is the source entry point for those shared settings.

An account records banking activity. Dashboards and reports summarize the recorded activity rather than replacing the underlying documents and transactions; see [banking](banking.md), [reporting](reporting.md), and [administration](administration.md).

## Related concepts

The main relationships are: company owns the working data context; customer -> invoice -> payment; vendor -> bill -> payment. An item can appear on sales and purchase documents, while an account records banking activity.

Categories, currencies, and taxes classify or value activity across domains. Dashboards and reports summarize the recorded activity. The [roles and permission levels reference](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/users-and-roles/roles-and-permission-levels/) provides the source context for access to those product areas; see [sales](sales.md), [purchases](purchases.md), [banking](banking.md), [reporting](reporting.md), and [administration](administration.md) for the domain detail.
