One horizon at a time." in [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md): a two-major upgrade is the exercise, and a third major is scope creep. Section 5 records what that choice costs rather than arguing it — 12's bug-fix window closed on 13 August 2026 and it is security-only until 24 February 2027 while upstream ships 13.25.0, so Ch3 lands on a release already leaving active support, with most of the clock spent. The audit's finding on what a later 12 → 13 move would face is kept as intelligence for whoever picks it up: 27 of the 30 `supports-12` packages already carry a ceiling of 13, and the two that do not are the first-party `akaunting/laravel-language` and `akaunting/laravel-mutable-observer`, both last released 2025-12-16, before Laravel 13 shipped. Section 5 also corrects an error this plan carries: Laravel 11 does **not** require a Laravel 10 application to collapse its `Kernel` files into `bootstrap/app.php` — upstream explicitly advises against it — so the real 10 → 11 cost sits in the 38 floating-point migration columns, the 6 `->change()` calls and the Sanctum 3 → 4 middleware retarget, not in the skeleton. Verified by the scripts' own reconciliation checks, two Composer resolver probes, a determinism rerun, five packages spot-checked by hand, and `herd php artisan test`.
